Specifications

Built to a standard.

Every material is specified, tested and documented. The full specification sheet is available on request.

Structure & facade

RCC framed structure with earthquake-resistant design, high-performance double-glazed aluminium windows, textured weather-shield finish and a ventilated stone-clad podium.

Living & bedrooms

Vitrified marble-finish flooring in living areas, engineered timber in bedrooms, 11-foot ceilings, acoustic glazing and provisions for split air-conditioning in every room.

Kitchen

Granite or quartz counters, provision for modular cabinetry and chimney, dedicated utility and service balcony, and dual water-point connections.

Bathrooms

Designer sanitaryware and CP fittings, rain showers, wall-hung WCs, anti-skid tiling and concealed plumbing with hot and cold lines.

Electrical & automation

Concealed copper wiring, modular switches, smart-home ready conduits, video door phone, EV-charging provision and 100% power backup for common areas.

Safety & sustainability

Fire-fighting systems, three-tier security, rainwater harvesting, sewage treatment with recycled water for landscaping, and rooftop solar for common lighting.
